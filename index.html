✨ Our Story & Philosophy

Where Tradition Meets Contemporary Pure-Veg Dining 🌿

Celebrating Pune's authentic palate with passion, warmth, and purity.

For years, Ishan Pure Veg Restaurant has earned its place as "Khavaiyye Punekars' Veg Favourite." We celebrate authentic multi-cuisine artistry with an unwavering dedication to 100% vegetarian culinary perfection.

From steaming morning South Indian filter coffee and dosas to rich North Indian curries, smoky Punjabi tandoor kebabs, and zesty Indo-Chinese sizzlers, every plate is prepared fresh with hand-ground spices and pure ghee/butter. Whether you’re stopping by for a quick breakfast, a relaxed family lunch, or celebrating an evening in our spacious indoor AC or breezy outdoor seating, Ishan welcomes you home.
